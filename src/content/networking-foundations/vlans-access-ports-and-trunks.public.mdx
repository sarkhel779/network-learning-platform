<h2 id="why-vlans-exist">Why VLANs exist</h2>

VLANs divide one physical switching environment into separate logical Layer 2 broadcast domains.

<h2 id="access-ports-and-membership">Access ports and membership</h2>

An access port assigns ordinary endpoint traffic to one operational VLAN.

<h2 id="trunks-and-802-1q">Trunks and 802.1Q</h2>

A trunk preserves VLAN identity while carrying multiple VLANs between network devices.
