<h2 id="assessment-overview">Assessment overview</h2>

This final quiz checks the introductory ideas from Computer Network Basics: why networks exist, host and device roles, addressing formats, and the OSI and TCP/IP models.

Sign in with a free account to take the remaining assessment and save the result. The assessment contains eight questions. A lesson is completed when its required quiz answers are correct; there is no manual completion or restart button.
