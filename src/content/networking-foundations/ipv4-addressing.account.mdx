<h2 id="inspect-address-evidence">Inspect address evidence</h2>

Use **Windows ipconfig** or `Get-NetIPConfiguration`, and **Linux ip address**, to verify the address and prefix on the interface that actually carries the traffic. Record the interface, address, prefix, gateway, and source of configuration. Do not assume that seeing an address proves the gateway or destination is reachable.

<h2 id="troubleshoot-addressing">Troubleshoot addressing</h2>

1. Confirm the active interface and its operational state.
2. Validate every octet and the prefix length.
3. Decide whether the destination is on-link or requires a gateway.
4. Compare with a working neighbour instead of guessing.
5. Treat a `169.254.x.x` address as evidence to investigate automatic configuration—not as the root cause by itself.

<InterviewScenario title="The address looks right but the host is isolated">A workstation has `10.20.30.40/16`, while its peers use `/24`. Explain how the longer perceived on-link scope can prevent it from sending some destinations to the gateway, then identify the smallest safe correction.</InterviewScenario>
