<h2 id="remote-access-capture-analysis">Remote-access Wireshark analysis</h2>

Identify the SSH protocol banner, negotiated direction, and host-key evidence without claiming that TCP 22 alone authenticates the server. Contrast this with readable Telnet negotiation and payload, and determine whether failure occurs before identification, during negotiation, or at user authentication.
