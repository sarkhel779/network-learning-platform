<h2 id="dnssec-and-advanced-wireshark">DNSSEC and advanced Wireshark analysis</h2>

Trace the chain **DS → DNSKEY → RRSIG** and use **NSEC/NSEC3** for authenticated denial awareness. DNSSEC authenticates data; it does not encrypt the query. In Wireshark correlate IDs, streams, retransmissions, TC fallback, EDNS OPT data, DNSSEC records, delegation, and timing rather than trusting one flag alone.

<h2 id="advanced-dns-operations">Advanced DNS operations and failure boundaries</h2>

Diagnose lame or cyclic delegation, missing or unsafe glue, bailiwick boundaries, cache-poisoning defenses, split-horizon answers, conditional-forwarding loops, and anycast path changes. AXFR and IXFR transfer zones over TCP; NOTIFY and dynamic updates have separate authorization and operational risks. EDNS can advertise larger UDP payloads, but fragmentation and path MTU still matter.

Encrypted transports—DoT, DoH, and DoQ—protect specified hops. They do not make the selected resolver blind, guarantee encrypted upstream resolution, or substitute for DNSSEC validation.
