<h2 id="cold-warm-cache-practice">Cold-cache and warm-cache practice</h2>

Compare the number of upstream exchanges and remaining TTL. A warm answer is valid only while its cached lifetime remains; a shorter path is evidence of reuse, not proof that authoritative servers disappeared.

<h2 id="record-selection-practice">Record-selection and delegation practice</h2>

Choose the record that answers the actual question: A/AAAA for addresses, CNAME for an alias, MX for mail routing, SRV for service location, PTR for reverse data, and NS/SOA for delegation and authority evidence. Treat glue as reachability help rather than the original final answer.

<SectionContinue itemId="dns_name_resolution_section_record_selection_practice" anchor="record-selection-practice" />

<h2 id="dns-packet-capture-practice">DNS packet and capture practice</h2>

Correlate `nslookup`, `Resolve-DnsName`, or `dig` output with Wireshark fields. Inspect `dns.id`, QR/AA/TC/RD/RA, RCODE, section counts, TTLs, UDP versus TCP port 53, retransmissions, and elapsed time.
